01 / About

I like
making
things.

Buildings. Interfaces. Illustrations. Graphics. Stories. Sometimes I'm not even sure what category something belongs in—and that's what I like about design.

Hi, I'm Eshani—an architecture student and multidisciplinary creative based in Georgia. I'm interested in the intersection of architecture, technology, art, and visual storytelling, with work that moves between physical and digital spaces.

My work spans architecture, UI/UX, graphic design, illustration, and content creation. I enjoy exploring how different ways of thinking and making can connect across disciplines, and finding new ways to bring ideas to life.

I'm drawn to projects that let me experiment, ask questions, and turn ideas into something people can actually experience. Whether I'm developing a building, designing an interface, creating an illustration, or experimenting with a new medium, I'm always looking for ways to combine creativity with purpose.

Currently, I'm pursuing my Master's in Architecture at Georgia Tech while continuing to explore the many ways design can shape how we experience the world.

Always learning. Always making. Always experimenting.
