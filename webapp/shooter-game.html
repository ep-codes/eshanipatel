The project.

A 3D first-person shooter game developed in Unity where the player navigates an environment, uses weapons to attack enemies, and interacts with enemy AI.

The project focused on creating a functional first-person gameplay experience, including player movement, weapon mechanics, combat interactions, and patrolling enemy AI.
