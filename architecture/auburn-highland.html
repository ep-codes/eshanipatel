Auburn Highland

Project overview

About the project

Auburn Highlands reimagines and expands the historic 546 Auburn Avenue property into a mixed-use campus that celebrates the cultural legacy of Atlanta's Sweet Auburn neighborhood. The project consists of three interconnected buildings: a residential building, an institutional building, and a hybrid residential/institutional building that supports a variety of community and creative programs. Designed as a live-work environment, the development provides artist residences and studio spaces, fostering creativity while preserving the site's historic Victorian character. Flexible institutional spaces accommodate art exhibitions, workshops, community events, and educational programming, creating opportunities for collaboration and public engagement.
