The Artisan Commons

Project overview

About the project

The Artisan Commons is a community-centered market hall built directly above the Decatur MARTA Station, that connects commuters, residents, and visitors. The project celebrates local artists, makers, and small businesses through flexible retail spaces, workshops, exhibitions, and community gathering areas. Designed to encourage creativity and social interaction, the market features open circulation, abundant natural daylight, and adaptable spaces for seasonal markets, performances, and public events.
